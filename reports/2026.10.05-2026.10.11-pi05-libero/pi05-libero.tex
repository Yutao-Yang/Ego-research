\begin{abstract}
  This report documents a closed-loop reproduction of the released $\pi_{0.5}$
  LIBERO policy from Physical Intelligence, evaluated on a single A100 GPU with
  the official OpenPI implementation. Under the pre-registered main protocol
  (P12, the authors' default precision) the policy reaches
  $1935/2000 = 96.75\,\%$ over the four LIBERO suites, $0.10$ percentage points
  below the official $96.85\,\%$. No weights were retrained. A separate
  highest-precision control reaches $97.00\,\%$. The report covers the benchmark,
  the model configuration, the pipeline, the frozen environment, the aggregate
  and per-task results, compute and timing, six genuine teaching cases, a
  physically replayed drawer failure, two clearly separated classes of anomaly,
  and the limitations of the claim.
\end{abstract}
