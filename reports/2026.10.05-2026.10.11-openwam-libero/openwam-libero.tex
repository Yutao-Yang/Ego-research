\begin{abstract}
  This report documents a closed-loop reproduction of the released
  OpenWAM-$\alpha$ LIBERO policy on a single A100 GPU. Using the authors' native
  task protocol and published weights, the policy reaches $1985/2000 = 99.25\,\%$
  over the four LIBERO suites, $0.05$ percentage points below the officially
  reported $99.30\,\%$. No weights were retrained. The report covers the
  benchmark, the model architecture, the data pipeline, the frozen environment,
  the aggregate and per-task results, all 15 failed episodes, compute and timing
  figures, a genuine teaching case, and the limitations of the claim.
\end{abstract}
