\begin{abstract}
  This report consolidates two closed-loop LIBERO reproductions carried out on a
  single A100 GPU during 2026.10.05--2026.10.11. Experiment~A evaluates the
  released OpenWAM-$\alpha$ LIBERO policy under its native protocol and obtains
  $1985/2000 = 99.25\,\%$ (official $99.30\,\%$). Experiment~B evaluates the
  released $\pi_{0.5}$ LIBERO policy with the official OpenPI implementation and
  obtains $1935/2000 = 96.75\,\%$ (official $96.85\,\%$). Neither model was
  retrained. Each experiment is written as a self-contained section pulled in
  from \texttt{reports/}, and the figures are
  shared from \texttt{figures/}. The two results answer each author's own
  reproduction question and must not be compared as a fair architectural
  comparison.
\end{abstract}
